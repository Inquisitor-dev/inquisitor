# frontend/CLAUDE.md

Web client guidance (Next.js 16 App Router, Zustand, SCSS modules + Tailwind 4). The root `CLAUDE.md` holds the rules that hold everywhere (copy, `lang="tr"` uppercase, ids and labels that mirror the backend). Server rules behind the screens are in `backend/CLAUDE.md`; reasons and trial notes in `docs/tasarim-gerekceleri.md`.

## Structure

- The frontend calls the API through the typed client `api` in `src/shared/api/client.ts` (`api.get/post/put/patch/delete`). It adds the store's auth token, parses JSON and throws `ApiError` on failure. URLs go through `apiUrl()` (`src/config/api.ts`), which defaults to `/api`; `next.config.ts` rewrites `/api/*` → `http://127.0.0.1:3001/*`. Use `logger` (`src/shared/lib/logger.ts`) instead of `console`.
- **Folder layout:** pages in `src/app/` keep their page-only parts in `components/` next to the page (e.g. `app/map/components/`, `app/menu/components/`); shared UI is in `src/components/`; per-universe static data is split by universe under `src/config/` (`interiors/`, `dialogue/`, `locations/`, plus `scenarioRegistry.ts`); audio settings are in `src/features/audio/`.
- Pages are under `src/app/` (`menu`, `map`, `interior/[locationId]`, `interact/[npcId]`, `crime-scene`, `result`, `market`, `community`, …).
- Game/auth state is a persisted Zustand store (`src/store/useGameStore.ts`) composed of slices in `src/store/slices/` (auth, session, evidence, inventory; shared types in `slices/types.ts`), including the session's `evidence` list. `createEvidenceSlice` drops `EVENT` items (night events are not evidence). The case length `lastDay` comes from the server and is read from the store (map, home, notebook).
- Per-universe config folders each have an `index.ts` registry; `interiorConfig.ts` / `dialogueConfig.ts` only re-export them. `config/scenarioRegistry.ts` + `shared/types/scenario.ts` hold the universe list (names, map backgrounds, footsteps). A new universe needs an entry in each.
- Mirrors of backend data that must stay identical: `config/locationLabels.ts`, `components/TruthRevealPanel.tsx`, `config/crimeScenes.ts` labels, the suspect list (`getSuspectsForScenario`) and the map's places (`minDifficulty` in `config/locations/`: the fifth suspect's place on every difficulty, the sixth from medium).
- Shared SCSS variables are in `src/styles/_variables.scss`; import them with `@use '../../styles/variables' as *;`.
- The **market** catalog is in `market/marketItems.ts`. `useMarketStore` (persisted) syncs the token balance, owned items and ledger with the backend `market/` module (`GET /market/catalog`, `GET /market/me`, `POST /market/purchase`, `GET /market/leaderboard`). The EUR token packs go through the Stripe test-mode flow in `payments/`.
- **Community** story and feedback submissions are mocked (`app/community/data/communityData.ts`); there is no backend for them.

## UI conventions

- **Visual language** (shared by menu, market and community): near-black background, blood red `#8A0303`, gold `#e0bb5e` for tokens and highlights, Playfair Display headings, wax-seal and roman-numeral motifs, and `lucide-react` icons instead of emoji (✠, ✓ and ✕ are kept as typographic marks).
- **Performance:**
  - No `backdrop-filter` (and no animated `filter`) on top of moving content, in the menu and in every game screen (dialogue, interiors, crime scene, home, notebook, board, dossier, abilities, map, wardrobe). Panels use a more opaque background instead.
  - Every in-game window carries `role="dialog" aria-modal="true"`. While one is open, the menu's looping animations pause (`body:has([aria-modal='true'])`), the 3D/sprite character stops drawing and the ambient particles (`useAmbientParticles`) stop drawing behind it (`isCoveredByModal` in `shared/lib/modalCover.ts`).
  - `CharacterModelView` builds its WebGL scene once and only swaps the model when the character changes (pixel ratio capped at 1.5); `CharacterTurntable` keeps the previous model on screen while the next manifest loads. The universe picker uses 480 px map thumbnails (`public/map/thumbs/`, `getMapThumb`).
  - Ember particles draw a pre-rendered glow sprite instead of canvas `shadowBlur`, and the home hints pulse a fixed glow layer's opacity instead of animating `box-shadow`.
- Themed screens (notebook, board, dossier) follow the universe through `data-theme`.

## Screens

- **Character stories and abilities:** `config/outfits.ts` holds each character's short `lore` and `quote`; `config/outfitStories.ts` holds the long story (paragraphs) and the abilities (name, summary, usage, rules). `components/character/OutfitStory.tsx` renders the ability cards and the "Detaylı Bak" modal in the market (cards and preview), wardrobe and lobby. Ability texts must never reveal the case answer and must match the server rules in `backend/src/abilities/`.
- **Abilities in play** (`features/abilities/`): `useAbilities` reads the session's abilities; `AbilityControls` renders the ability chips, the target picker and the result sheet. The map shows most abilities (Kulis and Çatı Geçişi are armed, then used when entering a location), the dialogue screen shows the ones aimed at that suspect, and the board has Veri Sızdırma (click a string's knot). The ability chips (`.dock` in `features/abilities/Abilities.module.scss`) sit bottom right above the crime scene and home buttons on desktop (not bottom centre or top right, where they cover map labels); on phones they stay above the bottom bar.
- **Map HUD** (`app/map/components/MapHud`, `MapActionBar`): the top bar has the exit, the town name over a day clock ("Gün II / IV", the five times of day as icons from `TIME_LABELS`, the current one named) and the daily question quota with a meter (`maxDailyDialogues`). The bottom bar has the tools with lucide icons (the explore link is hidden on phones), the wax-seal "Mahkûmu Seç" in the middle (on phones a round seal that rises above the bar) and the crime scene and home plates; at night the home button pulses with an opacity-only glow. Tool names turn to icons below 1280 px and to short names on phones. On phones both bars are hidden while a window (`aria-modal`) is open, since they sit above the windows.
- **Closed places and the paper:** the map marks today's `closedToday` places "Bugün kapalı" and does not walk there (`features/newspaper/useClosedToday.ts`). The morning paper is shown once per morning (`features/newspaper/useMorningPaper.ts`, `components/Newspaper`): parchment notice in medieval, China and winter, a newspaper clipping in modern, a tablet in cyberpunk. The notebook shows night events as a stamped "OLAY" card on that day's page.
- **Night raid** (`/raid`, `components/RaidScene`): each room's own picture (`public/backgrounds/home_<universe>_<room>.webp`, `getHomeRoomImage` in `config/homeConfig.ts`; drawn by day, prompts in `docs/ev-odalari-gorselleri.md`) darkened with CSS, a warm halo when the lamp is lit, the narration log, the actions and the turns left. Ending the day from the map or the home goes there when the response carries `raid.active`, and the map sends the player back to an unfinished raid on load. The client hides stolen items from confront, the board drawer, the verdict dossier and the notebook's photo drawer, and the inventory stamps them "ÇALINDI".
- **Detention:** the dialogue screen's "Nezarete Al" button, with a confirmation.
- **Crime scene:** the bottom bar's "Olay Yeri" button opens `/interact/crime_scene`, the narrator in the dialogue screen. Its name and background come from the session's `crimeScene` (store, `config/crimeScenes.ts`): `public/crime-scenes/<universe>/<id>_tidy.webp` or `_messy.webp`, following the scene's appearance. A picture that does not exist yet falls back to the old background. The picture is shifted up so the body stays above the dialogue card; for pictures whose body sits low, `CRIME_SCENE_FOCUS` in `config/crimeScenes.ts` holds the body's height and gives a bigger shift. The body is always covered so the picture fits every victim and way of death; the image prompts are in `docs/olay-yeri-gorselleri.md`. The labels in `config/crimeScenes.ts` must match the backend.
- **Evidence photos:** `ITEM` evidence is shown in the inventory (dialogue screen, map and home) as polaroids (`components/EvidencePolaroid`); the photo is the image of the place where it was found (`evidencePhoto` in `InvestigationBoard/boardModel.ts`). The crime scene trace and the body use the case's crime scene picture, the threat letter the player's home.
- **Verdict dossier (Hüküm Dosyası)** (`components/VerdictDossier`, `features/verdict/`): the map's "Mahkûmu Seç" (also `/map?hukum=1` from the home's bed) and the dialogue screen's "Hüküm Ver" (with that suspect preselected) open a step-by-step form: culprit → supporting evidence → weapon, motive, time → each other suspect's link → free-text account → seal. Its choices come from `GET /game-sessions/:id/verdict-options`; steps the case has no data for are skipped. It looks like a case file inside a folder whose colours and texts follow the universe (`data-theme`, `dossierTheme.ts`: medieval parchment with a wax seal, a manila folder in modern, a restricted record in cyberpunk; the office name, the classification stamp and the seal text). The step tabs on the folder's edge can be clicked once a culprit is chosen. Weapon, motive, time and each other suspect's link are blanks on the paper (`components/BlankField`): clicking one opens its choices under it, the answer is written in the handwriting font (long motives by their short name, `shortLabel`), and a suspect's link is shown as a red stamp. Sealing presses a stamp over the page before the verdict is sent. `condemnCase`/`finishCase` send it and store the result as `caseOutcome`; the result page shows it with `app/result/components/VerdictReportCard`: the same paper (`components/VerdictDossier/_paper.scss`, `paper-theme` mixin) corrected in red pen, with the player's answers in blue handwriting, correct ones ticked, wrong ones struck through with the truth written next to them, points in the margin, the total circled and the judge's comment as a margin note. Below it `app/result/components/SolutionPathsPanel` fetches `GET /game-sessions/:id/solution` and lists the evidence chains that would have solved the case, with the evidence the player found ticked; it shows nothing if the request fails or the case has no plan.
- **Dialogue screen** (`interact/[npcId]`, `components/DialogueScreen`) has the "Kanıt Göster" picker (`components/ConfrontModal`, calls `/npcs/confront`; already shown items are disabled) and a fear indicator under the NPC name. Its layout is final: the v2 idea of a three-part dialogue with the player character beside the chat was dropped, so don't add a player portrait to it.
  - "Hazır Sorular" toggles a row of preset questions (`config/quickQuestions.ts`, `getQuickQuestions`) that are sent with one click; the introduction uses the player's username and the town name. Sending one keeps the player's half-typed draft. Hidden while searching a location and at the crime scene.
- **Interiors** (`interior/[locationId]`, `InteriorViewer`, `config/interiors/<universe>.ts`, looked up with `getInterior`) are defined per universe; `medieval`, `cyberpunk`, `china`, `winter` and `modern` have them; other universes show a "henüz hazır değil" screen.
  - Entering a location from the map opens its interior first when one exists, otherwise the dialogue screen.
  - Hotspot positions are percentages of the image. A hotspot's icon comes from its category (`InteriorViewer/HotspotGlyph.tsx`: talk, search spot, clue, object, passage); configs hold no emoji.
  - A `clue` hotspot without `clueSnippet` is a search spot: it checks the warrant and links to `/interact/<id>?ara=1`, which opens investigation mode.
- **Player home** (`/home`, `components/PlayerHome`, `config/homeConfig.ts`) replaces the map's "Evim" popup in universes that have a home image (all five so far; a universe without one keeps the popup).
  - The room is the home's bedroom picture (`home_<universe>_bedroom.webp`, the same one the raid starts in); the spots and the character's position are tuned to it.
  - Objects in the room open the old popup's actions: bed (end day; on the last day it opens the verdict picker via `/map?hukum=1`), desk (the notebook), chest (inventory), board (opens the investigation board) and door (back to the map).
  - The equipped wardrobe character stands in the room, drawn from one frame of its 360° turntable sheet.
  - It shares the drag/pan and particle hooks with interiors (`InteriorViewer/useScenePan.ts`, `useAmbientParticles.ts`).
- **Notebook** (`components/Notebook`, opened through `CaseModals/NotebookModal` from the home desk, the map and the dialogue screen) is an open book with two pages and a 3D page-turn.
  - The first spread is the case file (scenario text) and the quick notes (`notes`); then one spread per in-game day, with ribbon tabs to jump between them. It opens on the current day.
  - Double-click on an empty spot (or use "Yazı ekle") to place a handwritten text box; text boxes, photos and record cards are dragged by their handle, also across the two pages. Changes autosave.
  - "Kanıt yapıştır" opens a drawer of the found evidence as polaroids; pick one, then click on the page to tape its photo there and write a caption under it.
  - "Kalem" opens the pen bar: three colors, thin/thick, an eraser that removes whole strokes and undo (also Ctrl+Z). Strokes are simplified before saving (`simplifyStroke`, Ramer–Douglas–Peucker) and drawn as an SVG layer over the page. On phones the tool buttons show icons only.
  - Cover and paper follow the universe (`data-theme`). On narrow screens one page is shown at a time and a horizontal swipe turns the page. The handwriting font is Caveat (`--font-hand`, loaded in `app/layout.tsx`).
  - Turning a page plays a paper rustle synthesized with Web Audio (`features/audio/pageSound.ts`); it follows the door-sound volume and mute setting. A short help card opens on the first visit (remembered in `localStorage` under `inquisitor-notebook-help-seen`) and from the "?" button.
- **Investigation board** (`/board`, `components/InvestigationBoard`) opens from the home's board.
  - The player pins suspects, found evidence and their own notes from the drawer, then drags from a pin to another card to stretch a string and chooses its meaning.
  - The board autosaves. "Düşün" shows the inner monologue, seals correct strings and stamps AKLANDI on a suspect cleared by a correct `CLEARS` string.
  - The board's material follows the universe (`data-theme`). On phones it is wider than the screen and scrolls sideways.
